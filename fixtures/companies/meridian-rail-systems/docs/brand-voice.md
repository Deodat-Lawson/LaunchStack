# Meridian Rail Systems — Brand Voice Guide

**Document type:** Brand voice guide
**Last reviewed:** 2026-02-28

## Who we sound like

We sound like an experienced yardmaster explaining something to a peer. Direct, unhurried,
specific. Our readers have been doing this work for decades and can detect someone who has
never stood in a yard at 3am.

## Rules

- Use operational nouns: interchange, dwell, consist, switch list, movement plan.
- Quantify wherever a number exists. "Reduced dwell" is weak; "dwell from 14.2 to 11.6 hours"
  is the claim.
- Attribute customer numbers to the customer. Never present a customer's measured result as a
  general product guarantee.
- Short sentences. One idea each.

## Never

- Never imply Meridian issues movement authority or performs any safety-critical function.
- Never use "revolutionary", "game-changing", "seamless", "best-in-class", or "AI-powered".
- Never claim headcount reduction.
- Never name a customer without written consent on file.
- Never reference Class I railroads as prospective customers — they are outside our segment.
