# Pilot reporting definitions
Fictional Harborlight Analytics document. Author: Ada Brooks, Finance Operations.
Effective: November 9, 2026, 18:00 UTC. Applies to Atlas weekly reporting.

## Populations
A contracted pilot organization has a signed pilot agreement. An enabled organization has the Atlas feature flag on. An invited account has received an onboarding invitation. An active account opened at least one saved view within the stated reporting window. Counts must name their population and window; do not divide accounts by organizations and call the result adoption.

## Commercial meaning
Pilot usage is not recognized revenue. An executed paid order and the applicable service period are required for revenue reporting. Atlas pilot agreements carry no standalone subscription charge this month. A sales demonstration is not an order, an invoice, or a collection.

## Weekly review handling
Quote raw numerator and denominator together if the dashboard definition is unresolved. Finance has not certified the pilot dashboard. Any account-based rate must use accounts in both numerator and denominator. This note defines terms; it does not validate event instrumentation or assert that invited accounts represent all eventual users.
