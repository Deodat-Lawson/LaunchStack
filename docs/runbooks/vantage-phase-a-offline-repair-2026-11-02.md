They become exact refs `doc:joseph-semantic/conflicting-metrics/finance@synthetic-v1` and `doc:joseph-semantic/conflicting-metrics/sales@synthetic-v1`. Both occur in retrieval `selectedRefs`, authorized registry, full frozen excerpts/content hashes and actual request `context.sources`. Native excerpt interning only replaces a duplicate string with an exact native text slice; Sales was not interned away or excluded.

The actual response recommends "Treat USD 12,000 as provisional, not final." Its only fact citations use the native intake copy, `conflicts` is empty, and its rationale says "This is the only current revenue evidence supplied and it expressly identifies unreconciled refunds." The exact report title, summary, rationale, interpretation, decision question, next step, fact/conflict texts and refs are unchanged by normalization.

**Therefore the failure is model selection/synthesis, not retrieval, serialization, version loss or normalizer deletion.** It is appropriate to retain the provisional label but insufficient to omit the differently defined Sales figure. Neither value is verified total revenue; a difference does not alone prove identical-definition contradiction.

The new diagnostic reports uncited exact refs and a scoped exclusivity-review signal. It does not turn uncited sources into facts, require irrelevant context to be cited, assert semantic relevance, force a conflict, or hardcode revenue values into policy. A single original plus a labeled copy, unrelated context, historical/quoted claims and no-op suggestions have regression coverage. Source-use diagnostics are not entailment validation.

Metadata date support is deliberately narrow English source attribution. A dated-source claim must occur in a cited fact and correspond to the checked headline/brief/recommendation attribution. The exact source version, snapshot/freeze hash and ACL match are required and sourceDate must be no later than all supplied report/snapshot cutoffs. A repeated unattributed event date cannot borrow nearby source-date support. Full actor/source freshness checks remain the existing upstream envelope/commit responsibility.

## Completeness and assessment authority

Previously `buildJosephEvaluation` described the pinned finite original document set as complete, while `attachLivePilotEvaluation` added bounded control excerpts and unconditionally marked it excerpted. This conservative downgrade, plus research STOP, explains the final needs-review gate. It was not proof that the reports were bad; conversely, lifting it would not cure Finance's critical omission.

`assessEvaluationCompleteness` now records reasons. Without an explicit trusted inventory, old calls stay excerpted. A **fresh** closed synthetic scenario may be declared complete only when:

1. The server/test host enumerates the finite full-source corpus before Generation, independent of Gold and model output.
2. Scenario/prepared-input identity, manifest identity and cutoff match.
3. The exact inventory matches selected, checked, used and supplied refs; full-content hashes equal the full supplied bytes and frozen source hashes.
4. Company/star requirements are present; no missing, revoked, omitted, failed, future, unauthorized or cross-scope source remains.
5. Retrieval is complete; research either completes or is explicitly declared unnecessary for that closed scenario and stopped without failures. This exception cannot conceal other partial/failing context.

The proof is returned separately from `prepareLivePilotContext` and is not part of the Generation envelope. `attachLivePilotEvaluation` accepts it only as an optional trusted internal argument and binds it to the prepared scenario. **Do not accept this proof from a browser, client, model or Gold.** It proves finite synthetic input coverage, not a complete workspace, factual support, policy validity, human review or a semantic pass. No-Judge and uncertain results remain unassessed. Old recorded inputs retain excerpted and their original scores/verdicts.
