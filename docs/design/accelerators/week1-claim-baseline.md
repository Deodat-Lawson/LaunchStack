## Labelling rubric

- **Unit:** a full prose sentence in `rawDraft`, counted once per occurrence in each section. A semicolon does not create another unit; the decimal in `0.4` is not a boundary. Multi-clause sentences must have support for all their substantive clauses. These are manual annotations, not a shipped sentence segmenter or a demonstration of the future claim-ID/hash algorithm.
- **Supported:** entailed by the actual numbered document excerpts and/or supplied fixture company-profile fields. This matches today's draft prompt, which permits both evidence and profile facts. Support is not the same as having a sentence-level document citation, regulatory approval, or independent confirmation of real-world truth.
- **Unsupported:** a company/product factual sentence with a substantive clause absent from or contradicted by those inputs. A plausible citation number alone cannot establish support.
- **Stale:** an explicitly superseded, expired or time-bounded fact used as though still current. The document's `2026-05-12` last-reviewed date alone is not an expiry date. There is no invented age threshold. No supplied source gives an expiry, successor or contrary dated observation for these outputs. In particular, the wording “current certifications” is supported relative to the supplied certification list, not independently revalidated as of October 19. The product's metric-freshness policy remains an open decision.
- **Input-inventory statements:** claims about what the supplied information does not contain are checked separately. They do not assert that the company has no revenue, pilots, other staff or qualifications in reality, and do not dilute the company/product factual-sentence denominator.

## Observations for the claims contract and Week 2

1. **Section-level citations are not claim-level provenance.** The current stage returns one `cites` array for the whole answer. C1's person/role and several product identities come from the profile rather than the numbered document. A later claim verifier must resolve the actual supporting profile/document reference, not treat an answer-wide cite list as proof of every sentence.
2. **A visible prompt-conformance issue remains.** Mission/product and team/compliance include bracket citations in `rawDraft` despite the production instruction “do not write brackets in the draft text”. The raw text above preserves this rather than silently cleaning it. This is a citation-format issue, not an unsupported factual sentence and not an implemented fix in this PR.
3. **Gaps were useful in this one sample.** The model reported the missing formal mission statement, commercial traction figures/periods and team qualifications instead of inventing them. The two prose statements about missing inputs are separately visible in the audit.
4. **Do not infer freshness from profile assembly time or document age alone.** No expired/superseded source appears here. The metric-freshness policy and the treatment of “current” claims backed by undated profile values still need the documented product decision; this report does not settle them.
5. **[PROPOSED] Week 2 trust work should distinguish three measurements:** actual indexed/scoped retrieval, claim-to-source support, and explicit supersession/dated-metric handling. Seed known unsupported numbers and explicit stale/superseded references when evaluating verification; this clean fixture-stage sample cannot replace those cases or justify X.

The [draft `Claim`/`ClaimVerdict` contract](../../../pipelines/src/proposals/verify/types.ts) remains exactly the proposed design shape, including `supported | unsupported | stale | conflict | unverified`. No segmentation, verifier, persistence schema, model prompt or runtime export was added. Person B's contract review is requested before subsequent claim-emitting draft/rewrite/editor work depends on it.
