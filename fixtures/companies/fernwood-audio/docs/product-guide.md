# Fernwood FR-2 and Fernwood Studio — Product Guide

**Document type:** Product guide
**Version:** 2.1
**Last reviewed:** 2026-04-05

## Fernwood FR-2

The FR-2 is a two-channel portable field recorder built for location sound. It records
32-bit float at up to 192 kHz to a single SD card. Because 32-bit float capture has effectively
no clipping point at the recorder stage, users do not need to set gain before a take.

Battery life is 7.5 hours on two AA cells at 48 kHz, measured with phantom power off.
With 48V phantom power enabled on both channels, battery life drops to 3.2 hours. We publish
both figures; marketing must not quote the 7.5-hour figure without the phantom-power caveat.

The body is machined aluminium and weighs 310 grams without batteries. It is weather-resistant
but **not** waterproof, and carries no IP rating.

## Fernwood Studio

Studio is the companion desktop application, available for macOS and Windows. It handles
transfer, metadata tagging, and a small set of restoration tools: broadband noise reduction,
a de-clicker, and high-pass filtering.

Studio is not a digital audio workstation. It does not support multitrack mixing, MIDI,
plugins, or timeline editing. Users move files into a DAW after cleanup.

## What we do not make

We do not make microphones, wireless systems, or mixers. The FR-2 has no built-in microphone —
an external mic is required.

## Supported use cases

- Documentary and interview recording
- Nature and ambient field recording
- Foley and sound-effects capture
- Podcast recording on location

## Known limitations

- No Linux build of Fernwood Studio, and none planned.
- No cloud sync; transfer is over USB-C or direct SD card read.
- Two channels only. There is no four-channel or eight-channel model.
- No timecode input or output.
