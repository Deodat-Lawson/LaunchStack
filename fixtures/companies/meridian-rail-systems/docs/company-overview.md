# Meridian Rail Systems — Company Overview

**Document type:** Company overview
**Last reviewed:** 2026-02-11
**Audience:** Internal, sales enablement

## What we do

Meridian Rail Systems builds scheduling and yard-management software for short-line and
regional freight railroads in North America. We do not serve Class I railroads; our product
assumes a network of fewer than 400 route miles and a dispatch team of under 30 people.

Meridian was founded in 2016 by two former yardmasters and a control-systems engineer. The
company is headquartered in Duluth, Minnesota and employs 61 people.

## Products

We ship two products.

**Meridian Dispatch** is our scheduling engine. It ingests interchange commitments, crew
availability and track-occupancy data, and produces a conflict-checked movement plan for the
next 72 hours. Dispatchers adjust the plan directly; the engine re-solves affected segments
without discarding manual edits.

**Meridian Yard** manages classification and switching inside a single yard. It tracks car
placement, generates switch lists, and reconciles against the railroad's existing car
inventory system.

The two products share an account but are licensed separately.

## Who buys from us

Our buyer is almost always the Director of Operations or a General Manager at a short-line
holding company. The day-to-day users are dispatchers and yardmasters. Purchases are
typically triggered by a failed audit, a retiring dispatcher whose knowledge was never
documented, or an interchange partner imposing new data requirements.

## Where we operate

Meridian operates in the United States and Canada only. We have no European or Asian
customers and no localisation beyond English and Canadian French.

## What we are not

We are not a positive train control (PTC) vendor. We do not provide safety-critical signalling,
and Meridian Dispatch is explicitly advisory — it never issues movement authority. Any
marketing that implies otherwise is incorrect and creates regulatory exposure.
