# Northwind Cold Chain — Compliance Summary and Approved Claims

**Document type:** Security and compliance summary
**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-12
**Owner:** Regulatory Affairs
**Status:** All external marketing claims must be drawn from this document.

## What Northwind provides

Northwind Cold Chain supplies temperature-monitoring hardware and reporting software for
chilled and frozen food logistics. Our sensors log temperature at fixed intervals and our
software produces the exception reports auditors ask for.

## Certifications actually held

- **ISO 9001:2015** — quality management. Certified since 2021, current.
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** — information security. Certified since 2024, current.

That is the complete list. Northwind is **not** ISO 22000 certified. We are not a certifying
body, and we do not hold accreditation to certify anyone else.

## What our customers use us for

Our customers operate HACCP plans. Northwind supplies the monitoring records that support a
customer's own HACCP plan. Northwind does not create, own, approve or validate that plan.

The distinction matters and is not optional in copy:

- Approved: "supports your HACCP monitoring requirements"
- Approved: "produces the temperature records auditors request"
- **Prohibited**: "HACCP certified"
- **Prohibited**: "makes you compliant"
- **Prohibited**: "guarantees you pass your audit"

## Measurement accuracy

The NW-40 probe is accurate to ±0.4 °C across the range −30 °C to +25 °C. Outside that range
accuracy is not specified and must not be claimed. The stated accuracy is the probe accuracy
only; it excludes placement error, which is the customer's responsibility.

Logging interval is configurable between 1 and 60 minutes. Default is 15 minutes.

## Data retention

Records are retained for 26 months by default. Retention can be extended to 7 years on the
Enterprise tier. We do not claim any specific regulatory retention period is satisfied by
this, because required periods vary by jurisdiction and product class.

## Required qualification in regulated copy

Any marketing that references audits, compliance or food safety must include a qualification
that Northwind supports the customer's own programme rather than replacing it. The approved
short form is:

> Northwind supports your monitoring requirements; your HACCP plan remains yours.

## Geographic availability

Northwind sells in the United Kingdom, Ireland and the Netherlands. We do not sell into the
United States, and our reports are not formatted for FDA submission.
