# Case study — Cedar Ridge Terminal Railway

**Document type:** Customer case study
**Published:** 2026-01-20
**Customer consent:** Named use approved in writing 2026-01-14

## Background

Cedar Ridge Terminal Railway operates 180 route miles across eastern Ohio with a dispatch
team of nine. Before Meridian, scheduling ran on a shared spreadsheet maintained by a single
senior dispatcher who was two years from retirement.

## What changed

Cedar Ridge deployed Meridian Dispatch in April 2025. Over the following nine months:

- On-time interchange performance rose from 71% to 88%.
- Average yard dwell fell from 14.2 hours to 11.6 hours.
- Plan rebuild time after a disruption dropped from roughly 40 minutes of manual work to
  under one minute.

These figures are Cedar Ridge's own, measured against their internal interchange log. We have
not independently audited them and they are specific to a 180-mile network.

## What did not change

Crew headcount was unchanged. Cedar Ridge explicitly did not reduce staff, and we do not
market Meridian as a headcount-reduction tool.

## Quote
